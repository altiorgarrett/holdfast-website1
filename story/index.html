Why Holdfast exists

A business can become stronger without becoming less human.

Holdfast is being built from a lifetime spent inside independent companies—watching remarkable owners create opportunity, carry too much, and face systems that often reward selling more than staying independent.

Read the founding story ↓See the operating model →

Build the institution that preserves the greatest possible number of owners.

Not through nostalgia. Through better infrastructure.

Conceived through experience. Clarified in Modena.

Holdfast is the structure its founders needed in nearly every company they built.

Garrett Boatz has built across music education, software, security, creative work, and nonprofit leadership. The industries changed. The pattern did not: capable people could do extraordinary work and still be left carrying payroll, contracts, risk, technology, administration, and growth largely alone.

Years spent alongside vendors, workers, founders, institutions, and family businesses—and later traveling with Rachel across different economies—turned that repeated burden into a larger question: what if independent owners could share infrastructure and leverage without sharing ownership?

In Modena, studying the institutions and business families that shaped Italian cities across generations, the separate pieces became one coherent idea. Holdfast would not be another service company. It would be an economic home for independent ownership.

The constitution before the company

Power needs limits before power becomes convenient.

These commitments belong in the foundation before Holdfast ever earns the scale to test them.

I

Ownership remains the owner’s.

Holdfast grows by strengthening companies, not by accumulating quiet control over them.

II

Shared value returns to members.

The leverage created by participation must improve member economics and capability.

III

Human judgment remains essential.

Technology removes burden; it does not erase relationship, context, or responsibility.

IV

Data serves the member.

Collective intelligence can strengthen the network without turning owners into the product.

V

Authority stays accountable.

Controls, transparency, and governance should be built before anyone believes they are necessary.

VI

We think in generations.

The deepest measure is how many healthy companies can continue beyond a founder’s tenure.

The long view

Holdfast is larger than a catalog of business discounts.

03Founders’ note

There is room at the table

We know the vision will change. The promise should become clearer.

Holdfast is being shaped through conversations with owners, operators, specialists, and people who understand patient institution-building. We are not waiting for perfect certainty to begin, and we are not using momentum as an excuse to stop listening.

The first services may be practical and narrow. The horizon is not. We believe independently owned companies deserve a serious institution built around their continued freedom—and we intend to earn the right to build it one useful relationship at a time.

Garrett & Rachel Boatz · Modena, Italy · 2026
