Why Holdfast exists

Independent companies should not have to sell themselves to gain the advantages of scale.

Holdfast is being built so owners can share strength behind the business while keeping the business itself.

Read the idea ↓See how scale works →

Shared strength. Independent ownership.

That is the promise everything else must serve.

The pattern

Good companies need corporate infrastructure long before they can afford to build all of it.

Across education, security, technology, creative work, retail, and nonprofit leadership, Garrett and Rachel kept seeing the same problem: capable owners were rebuilding payroll, benefits, finance, technology, risk, administration, and outside expertise one company at a time.

Large corporations solve that with departments and purchasing power. Holdfast asks whether independent businesses can share those advantages without sharing ownership.

The promises

Growth should make members stronger—not make Holdfast harder to trust.

I

Your company remains yours.

Ownership, identity, judgment, and control stay with the owner.

II

The value returns to members.

Scale should improve economics, access, service, and capability.

III

A department must be real.

Logos and referral links are not enough.

IV

Recommendations serve the member.

Provider compensation cannot buy the recommendation.

V

Professional work stays qualified.

Specialists remain responsible for their professional judgment.

VI

We build for durability.

The goal is stronger independent companies that can last.
