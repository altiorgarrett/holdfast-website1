The Holdfast operating foundation

The work behind a healthy company should not all land on the owner’s desk.

Holdfast is designing a practical service network for the everyday systems that determine whether a company can hire well, operate safely, and grow without exhausting the person who built it.

Employee benefits & experience

Give a small team a reason to stay—with benefits, wellbeing, and recognition that feel intentional rather than improvised.

Benefit platforms & enrollment specialists under evaluation

  • Perks, wellbeing, and recognition programs
  • Benefits design and enrollment support
  • Employee assistance and wellbeing navigation
  • Retirement-plan coordination
  • Team communications and annual enrollment

Payroll & people operations

A dependable operating structure for paying, onboarding, supporting, and documenting the people who make the business work.

Managed-payroll & HR partners under evaluation

  • Managed payroll and tax filing
  • Timekeeping and labor-cost reporting
  • Onboarding and offboarding workflows
  • Handbooks, policies, and HR guidance
  • Classification and multistate coordination
03

Workplace technology

The software, devices, and support employees expect—licensed correctly, set up well, and managed without another owner password spreadsheet.

Software, device, and help-desk partners under evaluation

  • Microsoft 365 administration and member pricing
  • Adobe Creative Cloud member pricing
  • Email, identity, and device setup
  • Help desk and lifecycle management
  • Cloud storage and collaboration standards
04

Cybersecurity & continuity

Practical protection for companies that cannot afford a security department—or the consequences of acting like security can wait.

Managed-security & continuity partners under evaluation

  • Managed endpoint protection and monitoring
  • Email security and identity protection
  • Employee awareness and phishing training
  • Backup, recovery, and continuity planning
  • Incident response and cyber-insurance readiness
05

Finance & controls

Reliable numbers and sensible safeguards that give owners clarity without turning their company into a finance department.

Bookkeeping, control, and advisory partners under evaluation

  • Bookkeeping and monthly close
  • Cash-flow and management reporting
  • Bill pay, approvals, and fraud controls
  • Tax coordination and document readiness
  • Fractional controller and CFO support

Insurance, capital & continuity

Relationships that help good businesses protect their future, handle risk, and pass what they built to the next capable owner.

Insurance, lending, and succession partners under evaluation

  • Insurance review and renewal coordination
  • Risk-readiness and compliance support
  • Capital and lending relationships
  • Succession and management-buyout planning
  • Family, employee, and independent-buyer pathways

The service promise

A service network with names, commitments, and someone responsible.

Holdfast will not hand owners a coupon code and disappear. Each partner relationship is expected to carry a service standard, member context, and a Holdfast lead responsible for how it feels to use.

Platform category

Employee experience

Perks, recognition, and wellbeing tools that can make a smaller team feel seriously supported.

Platform category

Productivity software

Microsoft 365, Adobe, and essential work tools that are licensed, deployed, and supported correctly.

Specialist category

People operations

Payroll, policies, compliance, and HR support that lets an owner lead people without becoming an HR department.

Specialist category

Protection & recovery

Cybersecurity, backups, insurance readiness, and continuity planning built around the risk a company actually carries.

These categories represent the operating model being built. Specific providers, pricing, and availability will only be presented as active Holdfast offerings after contracts and member terms are in place.
