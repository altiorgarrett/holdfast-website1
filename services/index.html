Shared corporate departments

Build the capabilities once. Make them useful across many companies.

Holdfast is developing common business functions that member companies can access without each one having to create, staff, negotiate, and manage every department alone.

A department is more than a vendor logo.

It needs a clear scope, qualified delivery, member workflow, implementation, service standards, escalation, and accountability.
01What members gain

The company behind the company

Capabilities normally associated with a larger corporate structure.

A member can use the department programs that fit its company, keep existing providers that already work, and add or change support when a gap is clear.

Holdfast builds the network-level structure: common standards, negotiated relationships, implementation pathways, provider accountability, and a record of what the company is using and what still needs attention.

02Initial department families

Start with repeated needs

Three department families behind most established businesses.

The first programs will be limited to capabilities Holdfast can deliver responsibly. Each family expands only when member demand, provider readiness, economics, and accountability support it.

01

People & workforce

The systems required to hire, pay, support, and retain the people who make the company work.

Initial program areas under development

  • Payroll and workforce administration
  • HR support, policies, and workplace guidance
  • Onboarding and offboarding workflows
  • Employee benefits and enrollment support
  • Perks, wellbeing, recognition, and training
02

Technology & security

Workplace technology and protection organized as a continuing business capability rather than a series of disconnected purchases.

Initial program areas under development

  • Business software and license administration
  • Email, identity, devices, and collaboration tools
  • IT support and lifecycle management
  • Cybersecurity, awareness, and monitoring
  • Backups, recovery, and business continuity
03

Finance, risk & administration

Shared structure around the money, records, protection, renewals, and outside expertise a company depends on.

Initial program areas under development

  • Bookkeeping visibility and financial reporting
  • Insurance and renewal coordination
  • Records, controls, deadlines, and administration
  • Purchasing and recurring vendor relationships
  • Access to qualified accounting, legal, finance, and risk specialists
04

Future department families

New capabilities will be built from repeated member demand—not from the desire to publish a longer service menu.

Future development areas

  • Purchasing and procurement
  • Travel and fleet programs
  • Sales enablement and training
  • Capital and lending relationships
  • Succession and ownership continuity

How a department becomes real

From coordinated access to a genuine shared capability.

Holdfast will not claim that every department exists before it does. Each program should mature through evidence and member use.

Stage 1

Coordinated access

Qualified providers, a clear member path, transparent economics, implementation support, and escalation.

Stage 2

Integrated department

Common intake, negotiated group terms, service levels, reporting, and dedicated relationship ownership.

Stage 3

Holdfast-built capability

Dedicated staff, technology, contracts, or operations when network demand and member value justify internal ownership.

Always

Member control

The company keeps final authority, may keep providers that work, and is never forced into a program simply to serve Holdfast’s economics.

Every capability will be labeled clearly as Available now, Founding-member preview, In development, or Future. Holdfast will not sell tomorrow’s capability as though it exists today.

03How a member uses it

Practical adoption

Network scale only matters when it becomes useful inside the company.

01

Map the current structure.

Holdfast records the company’s current providers, employee needs, spending categories, renewals, risks, and what should remain unchanged.

02

Choose the right programs.

The member sees which active department programs fit, what they cost, what may improve, and which decisions require approval.

03

Implement and improve.

Holdfast helps with setup, handoffs, follow-through, provider escalation, and the next department need as the business changes.

Your company remains yours.

Holdfast owns the shared system and the coordination process. Qualified specialists own their professional work. The owner keeps final decisions, spending authority, contracts, employment decisions, identity, and control.
