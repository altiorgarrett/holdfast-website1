What Holdfast looks for

A provider relationship members can trust.

Selection is based on member value—not on who pays the most for access.

  • Strong service and measurable member value
  • Transparent pricing and compensation
  • Capacity to support multiple companies well
  • Clear implementation and escalation
  • Qualified people doing qualified work
  • Willingness to be measured and held accountable
