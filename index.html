01The structural gap

Large companies build departments and buy at scale

Independent companies are usually asked to do both alone.

A healthy independent business may need payroll, benefits, HR, software, IT, cybersecurity, insurance, finance, purchasing, and professional support. It usually buys each function separately, with the pricing and attention given to a small account.

Holdfast changes the unit going to market. Member companies stay independently owned, but their combined employees, licenses, policies, purchases, and service needs create a much larger negotiating position.

What Holdfast builds behind members

Shared capabilities. Collective leverage. Practical adoption.

The goal is not a directory of vendors. It is a shared corporate backbone that independent businesses can actually use.

Picture a 25-person company

One company buys like a small account. A network of companies can go to market like a large one.

Today, the company may use separate firms for payroll, benefits, insurance, software, IT, and finance. Each provider sees one piece, and the owner still connects the work.
