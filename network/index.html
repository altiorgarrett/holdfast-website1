Why the network matters

One small company can be overlooked. Thousands acting through one institution cannot.

Independent businesses already spend enormous amounts on benefits, software, payroll, insurance, professional services, technology, and financing. They simply enter those markets separately, with different information and very little leverage.

Holdfast is designed to organize that demand without consolidating ownership. Partners gain a trusted route to good companies. Members gain the economics, standards, and accountability that normally follow much larger contracts.

The Holdfast partner standard

Access to the network comes with responsibility to the network.

Holdfast is intended to be more than a lead source. Partners share in a service architecture that protects member trust and makes strong performance visible.

  • Clear pricing and member economics
  • Named service ownership and escalation
  • Plain-language implementation
  • Secure, responsible handling of member data
  • Measurable service commitments
  • No quiet claim on the member relationship
