01The economic engine

Change the unit going to market

What is scattered today becomes a larger opportunity.

Independent companies already spend heavily on benefits, software, insurance, payroll, technology, finance, and expert services.

Holdfast organizes that demand so providers can compete for a larger relationship—and members can gain more than one small account usually commands.

Why it is not a coupon book

The network does more than find a discount.

  • Demand is measured and represented honestly
  • Providers compete for a meaningful relationship
  • Terms and service standards are clear
  • Implementation is part of the offer
  • Performance is visible across the network
  • Poor service can cost more than one account
The long-term possibility

A simple example

Thirty thousand employees do not have to work for one corporation to create the purchasing strength of thirty thousand employees.

They can work for thousands of independently owned companies acting through one trusted institution.

02What never changes

Scale without surrender

The network creates leverage. The owner keeps control.

Members can keep providers that already work and choose which programs fit their company.

Holdfast does not own the member company, its contracts, its people, or its decisions.

More members.
More leverage.

Every additional company can make the network more useful—so long as the value created comes back to the members.
